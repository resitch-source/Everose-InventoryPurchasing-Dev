📋 Section 1

System Overview

Everose ERP is a complete retail management system for home appliances and furniture. It runs entirely in a web browser — no installation required — and works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

What the system covers

🏪
Point of Sale
Fast checkout with barcode scanning, multiple payment types, receipts, and AR credit.
📦
Inventory
Real-time stock across 3 warehouses. Add products with photos, track movements, adjust counts.
🚚
Purchasing
Create POs, AI-suggested reorders, track delivery ETAs and partial receipts.
🏭
Receiving
Process goods receipts against POs, batch receive, auto-update AP ledger.
👥
Customers
Customer accounts, credit limits, AR aging, in-house and 3rd-party financing companies.
📊
Accounting
Double-entry journal, chart of accounts, AP/AR tracking, financial statements.
📈
Reports
Sales analytics, top products, supplier spend, inventory valuation, Excel export.
🔔
Notifications
Auto-alerts for low stock, overdue POs, high AR — sent via WhatsApp, Email, SMS, Viber, Messenger.

Making a Sale — Step by Step

1
Search or scan a product
Type the product name or SKU in the search bar, OR click the camera icon 📷 to scan a barcode. The product appears in search results — click it to add to cart.
2
Adjust quantities
In the cart, click the quantity box and type the new number, or use the + / − buttons. Minimum is 1; maximum is the available stock.
3
Apply a discount (optional)
Click the discount field and enter a percentage (e.g., 5 for 5% off the entire sale). You can also apply item-level discounts from the cart row.
4
Select the customer (optional)
Search for a customer by name or phone. Default is "Walk-in Customer." Selecting an account customer tracks AR balance and credit limit.
5
Click "Proceed to Payment"
The payment modal opens showing the total. Enter how much the customer pays.
6
Select payment method
Choose: Cash, Card, Mobile Wallet, Bank Transfer, COD, In-house Credit, or 3rd-Party Financing. You can split across multiple methods.
7
Complete the sale
Click Complete Sale. The system: deducts stock from WH-OZAMIS, posts a sales journal entry, and generates a receipt. Change amount is shown if cash payment exceeds total.
8
Print or save the receipt
Click Print Receipt to open the printable receipt. Your browser's print dialog will appear. You can also save as PDF.
⚠️
In-house Credit & Financing
When "In-house Credit" is selected, the sale amount is added to the customer's AR balance. Make sure the customer has a sufficient credit limit before proceeding. The system will warn you if the credit limit would be exceeded.

Barcode Scanner in POS

1
Click the camera icon 📷
Located next to the search bar. The camera will activate (grant permission if asked).
2
Point camera at barcode
Hold the barcode 10–20 cm from the camera. The scanner reads barcodes automatically — no button press needed.
3
Product added to cart
If the SKU matches a product, it's added to the cart instantly. If not found, you'll see a "SKU not found" message.

Deleting a Product

Receiving (GRN)

Receiving Stock for a Single PO

1
Go to Receiving module
Click Receiving in the sidebar. You see all Open and Partial POs waiting for receipt.
2

Batch Receiving (Multiple POs)

When a delivery truck arrives with multiple POs from one supplier, use Batch Receive to process them together.

1
Select multiple POs
On the Receiving screen, check the boxes next to each PO from the same delivery. Then click Batch Receive.
2
Review combined items
All line items from selected POs are shown together. Adjust received quantities for each.
3
Confirm all at once
One confirmation processes all POs, updates all stock, and posts one combined AP journal entry.

Accounting

The system uses double-entry accounting. Every transaction (sale, purchase, payment) automatically posts balanced journal entries to the ledger.

What is automatically posted?

TransactionDebitCredit
Cash saleCash (1000)Sales Revenue (4000)
Credit saleAccounts Receivable (1100)Sales Revenue (4000)
Goods received (PO)Inventory (1200)Accounts Payable (2000)
Stock write-offLoss on Write-off (6000)Inventory (1200)
Cost of Goods SoldCOGS (5000)Inventory (1200)

Viewing the Journal

1
Click Accounting in sidebar
The accounting screen shows the full journal ledger with all posted entries.
2
Filter by account or date
Use the filter tabs to view by account code (e.g., show only AR entries) or by date range.
3
View financial statements
The Accounting screen also shows: Trial Balance, Income Statement, Balance Sheet — updated in real time as transactions are posted.
✅
Fully Automated
You don't need to manually create journal entries. Every sale, receipt, and adjustment automatically creates the correct double-entry records. The accounting screen is read-only — it shows the results of operational transactions.

❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

General

Q: The system is slow. What can I do?

Close other browser tabs. Use Google Chrome for best performance. On mobile, close background apps. The system works offline — no internet needed.

Q: I accidentally deleted a product. Can I recover it?

If you have a recent JSON backup, import it via Data → Import JSON. Otherwise, the product cannot be recovered — it is permanently deleted. This is why regular exports are critical.

Q: Can multiple staff use the system at the same time?

Each browser/device has its own local storage, so changes on one device don't automatically appear on another. Use the Google Sheets sync or share JSON backups to merge data between devices. A networked version requires hosting on a server (see the Deployment Guide).

Q: The barcode scanner isn't working on my phone.

Make sure you've granted camera permission to the browser. On iOS, open Settings → Safari → Camera → Allow. On Android, open Settings → App permissions → Chrome → Camera → Allow. Try refreshing the page after granting permission.

Q: How do I change the store location for POS?

The POS is currently fixed to WH-OZAMIS (Ozamis City register). To change this, contact your system administrator — it requires a code change in the HTML file.

Q: I see "SKU not found" when scanning barcodes.

The SKU printed on the barcode must exactly match the SKU entered when the product was added. Check the product's SKU field in Inventory, and ensure the barcode label matches.

Q: Can I undo a sale?

Sales cannot be reversed automatically. For refunds, create a stock adjustment (write-in) to restore the returned units, and manually post a credit note in the journal. Contact your administrator for refund procedures.

Q: WhatsApp notifications open the app but don't send automatically.

This is intentional — the system opens WhatsApp with the message pre-filled, but you must press Send in WhatsApp to actually send it. This gives you a chance to review the message before it goes out.

Q: How do I add more suppliers?

Currently, suppliers are pre-configured in the system data. To add new suppliers, contact your system administrator to update the supplier list in the HTML file. This will be added as a user-facing feature in a future update.
